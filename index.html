About Me

Hello! I am Mukul Bhowmike, an adaptable IT professional and educator currently pursuing my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Uttara University. With a strong foundation built on a Diploma in Computer Engineering and specialized training in Cyber Security & Ethical Hacking, I bridge the gap between technical expertise and efficient management. Over the years, I have developed a diverse skill set ranging from core technical operations to academic administration. Currently, I serve as a Computer Operator & Exam Controller at Basic School & College, where I manage complex ERP systems, streamline student information databases, and handle high-stakes examination logistics with absolute secrecy and precision. My journey also includes hands-on experience as a Computer Trainer, where I have empowered others by teaching core technical skills, operating systems, and productivity tools. I am highly proficient in MS Office, AI tools like ChatGPT and Gemini, and thrive in fast-paced, high-pressure environments. I am a detail-oriented team player driven by ethics, integrity, and a passion for continuous learning. Whether it's managing data, ensuring digital security, or optimizing workflows, I aim to bring positive contributions and innovative solutions to every project I touch.

Smart City App: Your Ultimate Emergency Companion in Times of Need

In this era of digital transformation, new innovations constantly emerge to make our lives safer and more convenient. One such highly commendable and essential platform is the "Smart City" app. Designed to be a life-saving digital bridge, this app instantly connects users with nearby voluntary blood donors and crucial civic services like hospitals, police stations, and fire services. It has specifically emerged as an incredibly efficient tool for residents in and around the Savar area.

📱 Intuitive User Interface & Experience (UI/UX)

A glance at the screenshots reveals a clean, minimalistic, and intuitive design. The layout is structured in a way that anyone, regardless of their technical proficiency, can navigate it during an emergency. The main features are represented with clear, bold icons on the home screen. A prominent red "999 Emergency Service" banner allows users to immediately dial the national helpline with a single tap.

✨ Key Highlights of the App:

  • Hyper-Local Blood Donor Matching: Users can filter by blood group and find donors as close as 0.1 km away, with built-in options to call or WhatsApp them directly.
  • Location-Based Civic Directories: Utilizing real-time GPS, the app calculates the precise distance to nearby essential services (e.g., 0.9 km or 1.3 km).
  • Transparent Profile Tracking: Keeps a clear record of donors, including their last donation dates, ensuring safe and eligible intervals between donations.

💡 Conclusion: Why This App is a Must-Have

Emergencies and medical crises never give a warning. In the middle of the night, when looking for a rare blood group or searching for a police helpline, the "Smart City" app eliminates panic by providing organized, verified, and distance-sorted data. It is a beautiful convergence of technology and human empathy that everyone should keep installed on their smartphones.
